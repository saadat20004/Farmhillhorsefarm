Welcome to Middle Road

A quiet country retreat with room to breathe.

Big mature trees, open pasture and star filled skies. Wake up to fresh air and peaceful country views, then spend the day on the trails, at an equestrian event, or simply relaxing by the campfire.

Every site feels private, yet you are only minutes from Fair Hill, Newark and the Chesapeake. It is the perfect basecamp for hikers, riders, cyclists, families and RV travelers.
